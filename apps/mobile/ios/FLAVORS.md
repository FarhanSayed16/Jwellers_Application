# iOS flavor stubs (Phase 16)

Android `demo` / `ratnaraj` product flavors are configured in `android/app/build.gradle.kts`.

iOS schemes / bundle IDs for Demo (`com.yourco.demojewellers`) and Ratnaraj (`com.ratnaraj.jewellers`) are stubs for Phase 26–27 when iOS shipping is in scope. Until then, use Dart entrypoints:

- `lib/main_demo.dart`
- `lib/main_ratnaraj.dart`
